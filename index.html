    How it works

    Built so skill pays and grinding doesn't. Play as much as you want. Only getting better moves the needle.

    Get paid

    Every Monday the USDG prize pool splits by weekly points × rank multiplier. Your weekly points reset, your rank stays.

    Only your best counts

    Time spent means nothing, improvement means everything. Replaying a game only adds the difference over today's best. Farming easy runs, insta-failing, or resign-looping banks exactly zero.

    Hard daily cap

    Each game has a daily ceiling, and the three quests add a fixed +225. A perfect day banks 1,275 pts, whether it takes you 30 minutes or 10 hours.

    Weekly ladder

    Your weekly points reset every Monday, and the payout pool settles against them: your share = your weekly points × rank multiplier, over everyone's weighted total. Every week is a fresh race.

    Ranks are forever

    Lifetime points never reset. They set your tier, Bronze through Master, and your payout multiplier from 1.0x to 3.0x. Consistency compounds even when the weekly ladder wipes.

    /coin · 04

    $SKILL

    The coin behind the arena, launched on PONS. $SKILL is never handed out as a reward. Instead, every trade accrues creator fees, and those fees fund the weekly USDG prize pool paid to active players. No emissions, no inflation, no token dumps on players.

    Play daily → bank points Points → rank tiers Weekly points × rank → USDG prize
    Creator fees

    Every $SKILL trade accrues creator fees on Robinhood Chain. Those fees flow straight into the weekly USDG prize pool. Trading volume literally pays the players.

    Pre-funded treasury

    A USDG treasury funded at launch backstops the pool, so prizes are guaranteed through the early seasons regardless of week-to-week volume.

    Weekly settlement

    Fees plus treasury top-up settle into one USDG pool each Monday, split by weekly points × rank multiplier. Revenue in, prizes out.

    /fair-play · 05

    Anti-cheat

    Payouts only mean something if the board is honest. Cheating costs you everything, permanently.

    Humanly-possible ranges

    Every run is checked against human limits. Reaction averages under 90ms, aim under 120ms per target and typing over 230 wpm are voided on the spot. You see "RUN VOIDED" instead of a score, and nothing banks.

    Pattern analysis

    Input cadence, click intervals and run-to-run variance get profiled. Scripted play doesn't look like human play, and it shows fast.

    Settlement sweep

    Weekly prizes settle only after the ladder is swept. Flagged runs are pruned before a single cent of USDG moves.

    Permanent bans

    Confirmed cheaters are banned for good: wallet blacklisted from the leaderboard, payouts, and every future season. No appeals, no second wallets.
